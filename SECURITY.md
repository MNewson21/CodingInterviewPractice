## Reporting a Vulnerability
If you find a vulnerability while on the website or you spot something in the code, please email me at enquiries@codinginterviewpractice.dev 
<br><br> You can expect to get an update around 2-3 weeks after you have reported it. <br>
If the vulnerability is accepted, I will try and resolve it and will give you credit.<br>
If it is declined then I probably don't view it as super important and it can probably be omitted / left as is. Though if you think I am wrong, let me know<br>
